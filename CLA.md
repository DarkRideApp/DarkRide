# DarkRide Contributor License Agreement

DarkRide ships under a dual-licence model: AGPL-3.0 in the public repo, plus a commercial licence for customers who can't meet the AGPL's network-clause obligations. To make that dual-licensing legally sound, every Pull Request needs a signed CLA covering the contribution — without it, we can't accept the work into the dual-licensed tree.

There are two flavours depending on whether you're contributing as a person or on behalf of a company:

- **[Individual CLA (ICLA)](ICLA.md)** — for contributors whose work is their own and doesn't belong to an employer or other party. Signed once, covers all your future contributions. The common case.

- **[Corporate CLA (CCLA)](CCLA.md)** — for contributors whose work is owned by their employer (most full-time-employed devs, even for work done on personal time, depending on jurisdiction and contract). The employer signs and adds employees to Schedule A; those employees' contributions are then covered.

## How signing works

The first time you open a Pull Request, a status check from [cla-assistant.io](https://cla-assistant.io/DarkRideApp/DarkRide) will appear with a link. Click through, sign in with your GitHub account, and accept the ICLA. The status check turns green and stays that way for all your future PRs.

Corporate CLAs aren't self-service via cla-assistant — email **hello@darkride.app** with your company name and the GitHub usernames of contributing employees, and we'll set up the CCLA.

If signing isn't an option for any reason (the contribution is too small to bother with paperwork, you're at a company with no CCLA process, etc.) email us — we'd rather find a way to take the contribution than reject good work over process.
