## Reverse Engineering

DarkRide is a general-purpose mobile application analysis toolkit, in the same category as widely-used tools such as Frida, mitmproxy, and apktool.

- **Traffic interception** is performed via user-installed TLS certificates on user-owned devices. This does not constitute circumvention under the DMCA (17 U.S.C. 1201) or equivalent legislation, as no technological protection measure is bypassed — the user configures their own device to trust the proxy certificate.

- **Protocol decoders** (shipped as plugins) implement publicly documented protocols for interoperability. Clean-room interoperability reverse engineering is protected under EU Directive 2009/24/EC Article 6 and U.S. fair use doctrine.

- **APK analysis** (decompilation, resource extraction, binary inspection) is performed for interoperability and security research purposes on applications the user has lawful access to.
