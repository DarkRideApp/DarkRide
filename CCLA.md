# Corporate Contributor License Agreement

## DarkRide Corporate Contributor License Agreement

If your contributions are made on behalf of a company, your employer needs to agree to this Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA) in addition to (or instead of) the [Individual CLA](ICLA.md). The CCLA covers Contributions submitted by employees of the company who are listed on Schedule A.

This agreement is modelled on the [Apache Software Foundation Corporate CLA](https://www.apache.org/licenses/cla-corporate.txt) and uses similar language so corporate legal teams can review it quickly.

**By signing the CCLA via [cla-assistant.io](https://cla-assistant.io/DarkRideApp/DarkRide) on behalf of a corporation, the signatory agrees to the following terms.**

### 1. Definitions

**"You"** means the corporation on whose behalf this CCLA is signed, plus any of its Affiliates. "Affiliate" means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the signing corporation.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship — code, documentation, configuration, or any other material — that an employee, contractor, or agent of You submits to the DarkRide project through any of its public contribution channels (pull request, issue, gist, email, etc.).

**"Schedule A"** is the list of designated employees / contractors authorised to make Contributions on Your behalf. This list is maintained by You in the cla-assistant.io UI; You can add or remove names at any time, but removals only apply to Contributions made after the change.

### 2. Grant of Copyright Licence

Subject to the terms below, You grant the DarkRide project maintainers a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Contributions made by anyone on Your Schedule A and any derivative works thereof — including the right to relicense under any licence, including proprietary licences.

This grant specifically enables DarkRide's dual-licensing model: the same code shipped under AGPL-3.0 in the public repo and under a commercial licence for customers who can't meet the AGPL's network-clause obligations.

### 3. Grant of Patent Licence

Subject to the terms below, You grant the DarkRide project maintainers and recipients of software distributed by the project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this section) patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Contributions made by anyone on Your Schedule A.

This licence applies only to those patent claims You can licence that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution(s) alone or by combination of Your Contribution(s) with the work to which the Contribution was submitted. If any entity files patent litigation against You or any Schedule A contributor alleging that a Contribution constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licences granted to that entity under this CCLA terminate as of the date the litigation is filed.

### 4. Representations

You represent that:

- You are legally entitled to grant the licences above. If Your employment agreements or other arrangements with Schedule A individuals limit Your ability to grant these licences, You will resolve those limitations before listing the individual on Schedule A.
- Each Contribution submitted by a Schedule A individual is either an original work of that individual or properly attributed to the original author with all necessary licences and consents.
- You will notify the project promptly if any representation in this CCLA becomes inaccurate.

### 5. No Obligation

You understand that the project is under no obligation to accept, use, or include any Contribution. Acceptance is at the maintainers' discretion.

### 6. Support

You are not expected to provide support for Your Contributions unless You choose to do so.

## How to sign

1. Email **hello@darkride.app** from a company-domain address with:
   - Company legal name
   - Contact person (name, title, email)
   - Initial list of GitHub usernames for Schedule A
2. We'll set you up on [cla-assistant.io](https://cla-assistant.io/DarkRideApp/DarkRide) as a corporate signatory. A countersigned PDF is available on request.
3. Once signed, Pull Requests from any Schedule A username will pass the CLA status check automatically. To add or remove a contributor, email us — Schedule A changes are not self-service yet.

If you have questions before signing, send them to **hello@darkride.app**. We're happy to clarify wording or accept reasonable edits from your legal team — we use this CCLA verbatim from the Apache template specifically because it's well-precedented and most corporate legal teams have reviewed it before.
